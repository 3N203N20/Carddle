The basic rules of Daily Draw.

  • Start each week with 100 points.
  • You can draw once per day.
  • Your card changes your current weekly score.
  • At the end of the week, your final weekly score is added to your lifetime total.
  • Missed days cannot be played later.

Full Rules

How Daily Draw works, from your first draw to the end of each week.

1. Objective

Daily Draw is a daily card-drawing game. Your aim is to build up your Weekly Score by drawing one card each day. Cards can increase your score, decrease it, or leave it unchanged. Your score can fall below zero.

2. Daily draw

  1. Choose Choose today's card from the Home screen.
  2. Swipe left or right to browse the face-down cards.
  3. Tap a card once to select it. Tap it again to confirm your choice.
  4. The card is then revealed and its point effect is applied to your Weekly Score.
  5. You may draw only once per calendar day. After confirming a draw, you cannot change, cancel, or redraw it.

If you return to a result you have already drawn that day, you are only reviewing it. The score is not changed again. A missed day cannot be made up later.

All day and week boundaries are calculated using Japan Standard Time (JST, UTC+9).

3. Cards and rarities

Each card belongs to a rarity. Higher rarities generally have larger possible point effects. A card may have a fixed value or a value randomly selected from its displayed range. Positive values add points; negative values subtract points; Null cards have no point effect.

All cards have identical backs while you are choosing, so you cannot identify a card's rarity before it is revealed.

Rarity chances

These are the chances of drawing each rarity on a single draw, based on the current draw system. They are not the chances of drawing a particular card.

Each draw is random. These percentages describe the long-run probability, not a guarantee: for example, drawing 75 times does not guarantee that you will receive one God card.

4. Weekly score and Lifetime Total

  • At the beginning of each week, your Weekly Score starts at 100 points.
  • Each daily card changes the current Weekly Score. The score carries forward from one day to the next.
  • Your final Weekly Score is the cumulative result after the week's draws, including the starting 100 points.
  • When the week ends, that final Weekly Score is added to your Lifetime Total.
  • Your Weekly Score then resets to 100 for the new week.

A week runs from Sunday 00:00 to the following Sunday 00:00 JST. If you have not drawn on all seven days, the final score still carries over when the week ends; missed days do not pause the week.

5. Calendar and missed days

The calendar records whether you completed a draw on each date:

  • Green: you completed that day's draw.
  • Red: the day passed without a draw.
  • Grey: other days, including future days and days before your recorded activity.

You can switch between the weekly and monthly calendar views. The calendar uses JST dates.

6. Streaks

A streak counts consecutive calendar days on which you successfully draw a card. Missing a day breaks your current streak. Drawing again on a later day starts a new streak; it does not repair the missed day. Your longest streak is retained as a personal record.

7. Achievements

Achievements are unlocked by reaching the following milestones:

  • First Draw: reveal your first card.
  • Getting a Habit: draw on three different days in one week.
  • High Roller: finish a draw with a Weekly Score of 200 points or more.
  • One in a Million: reveal a Legendary or God card.
  • Rock Bottom: finish a draw with a Weekly Score below 50 points.

Unlocked achievements remain recorded in your game data.

8. Draw history

Draw History records your revealed cards, draw dates, week numbers, point changes, and resulting scores. It also displays your Lifetime Total. The history view shows your most recent 30 recorded draws.

9. Personal records

The Leaderboard's Personal Best section tracks your highest and worst completed Weekly Scores, highest and worst Monthly Scores, and longest streak. Monthly Score is the total of the completed weekly scores credited during that calendar month. A monthly record is finalized when the calendar month changes.

10. Guest data and online rankings

In the current version, your progress is stored in this browser on this device. Clearing the browser's site data or using a different browser or device may mean that this local progress is not available there.

Account sign-in, cloud synchronization, and global rankings are not connected yet. The Global Rankings area is therefore unavailable in Guest Mode. Your email address is not displayed as a public player name.

11. Settings and data

You can switch between Dark Mode and Light Mode in Settings. Reset game data clears the scores, draw history, and achievements saved in this browser. This action cannot be undone.
